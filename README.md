# Notes on *Topological Phases and Quantum Computation*

These are my personal study notes on

> Alexei Kitaev and Christopher Laumann,
> *Topological Phases and Quantum Computation*.

The goal is not merely to summarize the paper,
but to reconstruct the arguments from first principles
and fill in many of the intermediate derivations that are omitted in the original text.
Whenever possible, I derive the results directly rather than quoting them.

Topics currently covered include

- Majorana fermions
- The Kitaev chain
- Bogoliubov–de Gennes formalism
- Bulk spectrum and phase diagram
- Topological invariants (winding number and Majorana number)
- Bulk-boundary correspondence
- Majorana zero modes

## Status

This document is a work in progress and is continuously revised as I improve my understanding.
Corrections, comments, and suggestions are always welcome.
